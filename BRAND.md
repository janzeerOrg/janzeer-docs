# Using the Janzeer name and logo

The files under `brand/` are the official Janzeer marks. They are **not** covered by the CC BY 4.0 licence of the
documents; they remain the property of the Janzeer project.

**You may**, without asking:

- use the unmodified name and logo to refer to Janzeer — in articles, reviews, wallets, explorers, exchanges, integrations
  and educational material;
- resize the logo, and choose the variant that fits your background (`mark.svg`, `mark-white.svg`, `mono.svg`, the lockups).

**You may not**:

- change the mark's shape or colours, add effects, or combine it with another logo into a new one;
- use the name or logo in a way that suggests the Janzeer project endorses, sponsors or operates your product or service;
- use them as your own product name, application icon, domain or social-media identity;
- use them for anything deceptive, including imitating official Janzeer software or websites.

Clear space: keep at least the stroke width of the mark free on every side. Minimum size: 16 px for the mark.

Official builds are distributed only through <https://janzeer.org> and the repositories of
<https://github.com/janzeerorg>. Anything else is not from the project, whatever logo it carries.

Questions and permission requests: **janzeeer@proton.me**.

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `mark.svg` | the mark in the brand crimson, on light or dark backgrounds |
| `mark-white.svg` | the mark in white, on the crimson or on photographs |
| `mono.svg` | one colour (inherits the text colour), for print |
| `mark-small.svg` | heavier strokes for 32 px and below |
| `lockup-*.svg` | the mark with the name, Latin or bilingual, for dark and light backgrounds |
| `token.svg`, `token-square.svg` | the JNZ token face |
| `png/` | raster exports: app icons, avatars, social banners |
